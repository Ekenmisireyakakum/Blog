Hi, I'm Tanova

Writer, Developer & Lifelong Learner

I started Tanova Blog as a personal space to think out loud — to share what I am learning, what I am building, and what I find genuinely useful. Not polished content for algorithms, just honest writing for real people.

My background is in web development, but I write about a lot more than code. I am deeply interested in how people learn, how habits shape outcomes, and how small, consistent actions build remarkable things over time.

Every article on this blog is something I have either lived through, studied carefully, or built with my own hands. I do not publish things I would not want to read myself.

The Mission

Tanova Blog is a personal space for sharing practical knowledge, technology, experiences and ideas. The goal is simple: write things that are genuinely useful to real people, without the fluff that fills most content online.

Whether you are a developer just starting out, someone trying to build better habits, or just a curious reader — there is something here for you. I write slowly, carefully, and only about things I actually care about.

The blog will keep growing as I do. Every article is a moment of clarity captured in writing.
